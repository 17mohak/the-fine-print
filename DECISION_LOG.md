Trade-offs: cold-start latency, memory per active user, index loss on restart, repeated decryption, no cross-agreement ranking. Confidence medium (unbenchmarked). Reversibility high. Revisit triggers: validated cross-vault need; benchmark showing agreement-scoped retrieval insufficient or vault-wide ephemeral build exceeding owner-set SLOs. Spec: SECURITY_AND_PRIVACY section 5.

## Reading
- Strongest evidenced **pain + pathway**: insurance claim disputes. Strongest evidenced **deterministic regulatory checks**: bank lending (KFS, prepayment, cooling-off). These are different kinds of strength and should not be merged into one score.
- Weakest for a pan-India engine: rental (state law, bespoke documents, one-off use).
- Evidence gaps are not evidence of no problem for rental, lending comprehension, warranties.

# 10 — Consumer Validation Protocol

**Preparation only. No interviews, concierge cases or participant data exist.** Builds on WS02 §E and Doc 06 §C. Target: enough evidence to make a defensible C1-vs-C2 call, not a full market study. Stop rules below prevent drift.

## 1. Objectives (what the fieldwork must settle)
1. How people actually behave at each lifecycle stage (documents kept, read, shared, retrieved; who helped).
2. For C1: whether claimants whose claim was rejected/partly paid/delayed seek help, what they already paid for, and what happened.
3. For C2: whether borrowers/co-borrowers ever check or dispute fees/terms; violation frequency signals; documents they hold.
4. Whether any stronger problem emerges (rental, subscriptions, other) — challenger slots stay open.
5. Delegators: who manages documents for whom.

## 3. Recruitment
Outside the product's own channels: personal/community networks via a recruiter, consumer-complaint forums (ask moderators; no spam), local coaching/co-working communities, hospital/finance-adjacent community groups, a panel vendor for screening. Screener (below) does not mention AI or the product. Incentive: fixed amount disclosed upfront, not tied to answers. **User involvement:** you (or an agency) must own recruiting and consent; I cannot contact participants.

Screener questions: which of [claim in last 24 months, bought policy, loan, rented, subscription] apply; outcome of the claim (rejected/partly/delayed/smooth); city/language; who handled the paperwork; willing to show documents on screen (yes/no).

## 4. Interview script (45–60 min; neutral; behaviour first)
Opening (consent recap, no right answers).
A. **Event walk-through:** "Tell me about the last time you [bought/signed/claimed]. Start from the first thought." → "Then what?" repeated.
B. **Documents:** what arrived; where kept now; "show me where it is" (screen share, redacted view allowed); who else has copies.
C. **Before signing:** what you did with it, what you looked at and why, who you asked, what they said.
D. **Afterwards:** anything learned later; how; what changed next time.
E. **Problem event (if any):** what first happened; what you did next; who you contacted; how long; what it cost in money/time/effort; what the other party said and how you checked; documents hard to find; why you escalated or stopped; official channels considered and why.
F. **Tracking:** how you track renewals, dates, charges today (show).
G. **Spend:** what have you paid for help (agent, lawyer, firm, app) and was it worth it.
H. **Loans only:** which fees you knew about at signing; whether statement/charges ever surprised you; whether you ever compared the sanction letter to what you were charged; foreclosure/prepayment experience.
I. **Only at the end, separate segment:** show a neutral mock of an explanation or complaint pack; ask what they'd check before trusting it, what they'd do with it, what would stop them uploading documents. Do not ask "would you use / pay".
Probes: "What happened next?", "Can you show me?", "How did you know?", "What did you do instead?" Never offer answer lists. Two interviewers: one leads, one logs.

## 5. Consent and document-redaction protocol
- Written/recorded consent: purpose, voluntary, may stop, recording optional, retention period (default 12 months, then delete), who sees it, withdrawal right, DPDP-aligned notice (counsel to approve wording).
- Documents are **viewed on screen by default**; no files collected. If a participant volunteers files: collect only with separate consent, participant redacts name, policy/loan/Aadhaar/PAN/phone/address/diagnoses/account numbers before sharing (or we redact in a secure workspace with them present); store encrypted, access-limited, delete at project end; never use in tooling or tests without further written consent.
- Health data: record only what is needed; no medical details in notes; pseudonymous IDs (P01…).
- Do not give legal/claim advice during interviews; if a participant has an urgent deadline (e.g. Ombudsman 1-year limit), tell them official sources and stop the research role (referral list prepared).
- Counsel and any ethics reviewer approve before first interview (user action).

## 6. Evidence template (one per interview; fields)
ID · segment tags · date · language · interviewer/logger · domain · lifecycle stage · documents held (type, location, redacted-view seen Y/N) · timeline (dated events) · actions taken · helpers/intermediaries · official channels used/considered · costs (₹, hours) · outcome · what they did NOT do and why · verbatim quotes (with timestamp) · behaviour vs stated-opinion gaps · unprompted pain statements · spend history · trust/upload concerns · interviewer confidence (H/M/L) · coding tags (problem, workaround, trigger, barrier).

## 7. Recording contradictions
Maintain a **Contradiction Log**: claim A (source ID) vs claim B (source ID), type (stated vs revealed behaviour / participant vs participant / participant vs desk evidence), date, how checked, status (open/resolved/accepted-as-variance). Rule: contradictions stay visible in synthesis; any theme needs ≥3 independent participants and ≥1 disconfirming-case check; stated opinions never override observed behaviour; each segment's summary lists disconfirming quotes first.

## 8. Concierge validation protocol (C1 and C2)
Purpose: test behaviour and value with real cases **without building** — a human operator (you/analyst) with a checklist; no automation, no promise of outcomes, no legal advice.
**Eligibility (C1):** person with rejected/partly paid/delayed health or motor claim who wants help and consents; documents available; no counsel conflict. **(C2):** borrower/co-borrower with KFS/sanction letter/agreement and a fee, prepayment or exit question.
**Flow:** (1) consent + deadlines screen (state Ombudsman 1-year/1-month; RBI 90-day windows; tell user the free official routes and that the service is not legal advice); (2) document intake (redacted copy preferred); (3) operator completes structured worksheet: C1 — policy clauses vs letter reasons, missing documents, circular/Rules checkpoints (para 17(b) specific terms; moratorium 60 months; 1h/3h; Ombudsman eligibility), complaint draft; C2 — KFS vs sanction letter vs schedule of charges, fees not in KFS, prepayment rules by lender type/date, cooling-off, escalation route; (4) deliver pack; (5) record what user did next; (6) 30/60/90-day follow-up of action and outcome.
**Pre-register measures:** time to produce pack; operator confidence; user-reported usefulness; **action taken** (filed? paid? withdrew?); rule-library gaps found; errors caught by second reviewer; **payment test:** offer a fixed fee *after* delivering value (₹ amount set by user) and record accept/decline/negotiate — willingness to pay stays "unknown" until real payments occur; also record "would they have gone to Samadhan/agent/lawyer instead" with revealed alternatives.
**Cases:** 10 C1, 10 C2 minimum; stop early if first 5 show no action change.
**Safeguards:** second reviewer on every pack; no outcome predictions; no submissions on user's behalf; written disclaimers; counsel-approved template (user action); incident log.
**C1/C2 decision indicators (proposed, for user approval; none pre-judged):** share of cases where the pack changed the user's next action; share who file with an official channel; payment behaviour; error rate caught by reviewer; operator hours per case; count of cases where rules/documents lacked needed info. Thresholds to be set by the user before the first case.

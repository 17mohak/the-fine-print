## Approved amended stop rule (L7) — Gate 2 is NOT convened automatically
The evidence programme below must be completed and the results returned to the user for review before any Gate 2 decision.
1. **Regulatory reads (retained, high-impact only):** IRDAI Policyholder Protection Regs 2024 + its master circular (claim timelines, interest, grievance TAT); PED cap in Insurance Products Regs 2024; NBFC KFS/prepayment directions; CIO FY25 figures (OCR/manual); upgrade mirror/non-official copies to official-host copies where obtainable.
2. **H1 comparison (retained):** two general assistants plus available specialist explainers, 12 pre-registered scenarios, blind scoring (Doc 09).
3. **Consumer interviews (retained):** 24 genuine interviews (Doc 10 sample plan).
4. **Concierge validation (amended):** **5 genuine cases per candidate (C1, C2)**; expand to 10 only where meaningful signals justify it. **Pause a candidate if its first 5 cases show no meaningful action change**, and document the reason (what was tried, what the users did, why no change). "Meaningful action change" indicators and thresholds to be fixed by the user before case 1 (Doc 10 §8).
5. **Counsel (retained):** an *initial* opinion on Q-P1 (legal-practice boundary), Q-P4 (portal interaction), insurance-advice/distribution boundary, DPDP, and Ombudsman representative filing.
6. **Comparison (amended):** C1, C2 and any credible challenger surfaced by interviews are scored on the **same criteria**, with four dimensions kept explicitly separate and never blended into one score: (a) **consumer demand**, (b) **willingness to pay** (revealed behaviour only), (c) **technical differentiation** (incl. general-AI parity), (d) **regulatory feasibility**. Pain severity and prevalence are reported separately as inputs.
Deferred unless interviews surface them: rental state matrices, warranty, international comparators, financial models.

### Evidence-register convention (L8)
Each source in Doc 02 carries a host class: **VP-official** (read from the issuing authority's own host), **VP-mirror** (text read from a third-party host, e.g. RB-IOS 2026 via a bank site; Health Master Circular via caalley.com), **VS**, **SU**, **UNK**. Mirror copies are not equated with official verification; each needs an official-host re-read or a documented reason it cannot be obtained.
